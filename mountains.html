🏔 Emergence of Mountains

Mountain formation occurs due to a variety of geological processes associated with large-scale movements of Earth's crust (tectonic plates).

Volcanic Mountains

Movements of tectonic plates create Volcanoes along the plate boundaries, which erupt and form mountains.

Fold Mountains

 When plates collide or undergo subduction (that is, ride one over another), the plates tend to buckle and fold, forming mountains.

Block mountains

 When a fault block is raised or tilted, a block mountain can result. Higher blocks are called horsts, and troughs are called grabens.
