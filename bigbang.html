01 · Big Bang

The Big Bang theory explains how the universe began from an extremely hot and dense state around 13.8 billion years ago.

Expansion

Space itself expanded rapidly after the Big Bang.

Cooling

As the universe expanded, it cooled and particles formed.

Atoms

Hydrogen and helium atoms formed first.

Galaxies

Gravity slowly formed stars and galaxies.
